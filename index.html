Notes for the wanderer.

Lastlight: Woodland Cards is an offline survival card game. Gather supplies, craft tools and shelter, and manage your food, nerve, health, and fire through seven nights.

The first release is in development. An App Store release date has not been announced.

How to play

  1. Tap Woodland, Quarry, or Marsh to gather supplies. Every trip costs 1 daylight and 3 food.
  2. Tap a card to eat, heal, or fuel the fire. Open Craft to turn materials into meals, medicine, tools, and shelter.
  3. Open End day to see exactly what the night will cost. Prepare your camp, then face the night.

Progress and sound

Your journey saves automatically on the device. Starting a new journey replaces the old save after confirmation. Deleting the app removes its local data. Sound is optional and can be toggled using the music button.

Contact support

Send a support request through the Lastlight support tracker. Include your device model, operating system version, game version, and what happened. A GitHub account is required to submit a request.

Support requests on this tracker are public. Please do not include passwords, email addresses, or other private information.

Privacy policy

EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 2, 2026 · LASTLIGHT 1.0

Lastlight: Woodland Cards is provided by Xingze Wang. This policy describes how the game and this support website handle information.

The game collects no personal data.

The game does not send data to the developer or to third parties. It includes no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party SDKs. Gameplay works entirely offline and does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

Your save stays on your device.

The game stores the current journey and your sound preference locally. The developer cannot access this data. Starting a new journey replaces the current save. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Device or operating-system backups may be managed separately by your device settings.

This website and support requests

This static support site is hosted by GitHub Pages and includes no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub processes web requests as the hosting provider under its privacy statement. If you submit a support request on GitHub, the information you choose to share is processed by GitHub and appears in the public support tracker. Share only information needed to describe the issue.

Questions and updates

Use the support tracker for questions about this policy without posting sensitive personal information. If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
