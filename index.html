Notes for the wanderer.

Lastlight: Living Wilds is an offline woodland survival game with autonomous wildlife and permanent evolution choices. Walk through the world, gather supplies, craft tools and shelter, and build a lasting camp in a changing woodland.

The first release is in development. An App Store release date has not been announced.

How to play

  1. Use the movement pad or tap clear ground to walk. On a keyboard, use WASD or arrow keys. Tap a resource to approach and gather, or press E beside it.
  2. Open Craft to make tools, a torch, shelter, or a new campfire. Eat berries from your satchel and feed campfires with wood. Cook two berries beside a lit fire for a warm meal. Build a camp workbench to craft a pick, mine copper and make stronger equipment. The 21 recipes include a spirit focus, hide armor, a sun hat, two backpacks, roasts and trail rations. Maintain tools before their durability runs out. Wet clothes dry faster at camp; a lean-to shelters you from rain.
  3. Daylight fades automatically, and your journey has no fixed day limit. Find light before nightfall. Watch grazers forage, wolves hunt, and rain change your camp. Wolves mark a pounce before attacking: step aside or interrupt with a strike. Feed the curious fox two berries to earn a spirit bond and choose its evolution. Absorb a beast core from a core-bearing beast or the northeast ruins to evolve your body. The Evolution path button reviews permanent choices. Menus and leaving the game pause time.

Carry, hunt and equip

Open Pack, or press I, to manage supplies and choose your weapon. The starting satchel holds 10 stacks. A woven backpack raises this to 16; reinforce it with hide at a workbench for 22. Wild fruit shares this space. Put down one item or a stack when you need room. Overflow stays on the ground for four active game days; older over-capacity saves keep their contents.

Tap a grazer deliberately to hunt it: collect two small cuts and one hide. A wolf yields two raw haunches, one hide, one sinew, and a core only when that individual carries one. Cook meat at a lit fire; raw meat harms health. A spear spends stamina, while a moonleaf focus spends nerve on a traveling spirit bolt. Hide armor reduces bites and wears down. Body evolution changes appetite and the land can strengthen or suppress your affinity; learn Aura sight to inspect the current elemental effects in Pack and Fieldbook.

Explore and prepare

New journeys generate six broad regions with 18 connected local habitats, organic coastlines, rivers, branching roads, bridges and landmarks. Six tree and six shrub species respond differently to seasons. Golden berries become dormant in frost; highland cloudberries remain productive. The map reveals explored ground; select known land to place a marker and walk there. Marshes slow and wet you, highlands drain warmth, and ashlands drain nerve. Autumn, winter, spring and summer repeat in six-day phases. Winter brings longer nights and cold, spring brings frequent rain, and summer builds body heat. Standing leafy trees, a lean-to, a woven sun hat, rain and cooler hours help you stay cool. The clock shows changing daylight and warns before the next season. A reed-lined cloak reduces exposure, while a stone hearth halves fuel use.

Older saves preserve their existing geography, camps and resources. Start a new journey for the expanded habitat generation. New journeys begin in autumn in one continuous world. Older saves keep their original climate profile and current seasonal/daylight phase. Fieldbook explains all four seasons, local conditions, five elemental fruits and story traces unlocked by exploration.

Learn to read the unseen

Some animals carry a core; many do not. Even within one species, cores can differ. A compatible core can strengthen the host, while an opposing core can weaken it. This is separate from the effect of local ground. Before learning perception, you cannot identify these hidden properties.

After absorbing a core, open Fieldbook beside a built camp workbench and spend two moonleaf to learn Aura sight. It reveals the ground and creature body affinities. Then spend one absorbed essence to learn Core discernment and reveal core presence, type and host compatibility. Essence is held in the body and uses no backpack space. Learning preserves your permanent evolution choices. Individual cores, learned skills and essence balances are saved; loading does not reroll them.

Progress and preferences

Your journey saves automatically on the device. Starting a new journey replaces the old save after confirmation. Deleting the app removes its local data. Sound and reduced motion can be adjusted from the pause menu. A previous valid autosave can recover a damaged save. If another game tab saves a newer journey, the older tab pauses to protect progress.

Privacy policy

EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 3, 2026 · LASTLIGHT 1.0

Lastlight: Living Wilds is provided by Xingze Wang. This policy describes how the game and this support website handle information.

The game collects no personal data.

The game does not send data to the developer or to third parties. It includes no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party SDKs. Gameplay works entirely offline and does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

Your save stays on your device.

The game stores the current journey, a previous valid autosave, and sound and motion preferences locally. The developer cannot access this data. Starting a new journey replaces the current save. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Device or operating-system backups may be managed separately by your device settings.

This website and support requests

This static support site is hosted by GitHub Pages and includes no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub processes web requests as the hosting provider under its privacy statement. If you submit a support request on GitHub, the information you choose to share is processed by GitHub and appears in the public support tracker. Share only information needed to describe the issue.

Questions and updates

Use the support tracker for questions about this policy without posting sensitive personal information. If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
